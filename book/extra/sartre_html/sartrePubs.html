Sartre Publications

This page collects publications by Sartre authors that describe the generator, its calculations, or its use. Entries are ordered by first public appearance, newest first. The first five entries are the Sartre-related results from the INSPIRE Sartre title search; the final entry is the foundational electron–ion study.
