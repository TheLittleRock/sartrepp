Physics References

These publications by authors outside the Sartre collaboration provide essential physics input for the library: impact-parameter dependent dipole amplitudes, saturation, nuclear geometry, and coherent and incoherent diffraction. Sartre-specific publications are listed separately in Sartre Publications.
