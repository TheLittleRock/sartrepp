Sartre Talks

This list collects public conference presentations and seminars that describe Sartre, its applications, or its ongoing development. It is intentionally limited to publicly accessible records and is ordered newest first. Sartre journal publications are listed separately in Sartre Publications.

Presentations

  1. T. Toll, New developments in the Sartre event generator, MC4EIC 2026, 28 August 2026.

    A summary of faster table production, inclusive diffraction, and the dedicated UPC version.

  2. T. Toll and V. Nattoja, Describing UPC data with the Sartre event generator, 2nd International Workshop on the Physics of Ultra-Peripheral Collisions (UPC2025), 11 June 2025.

    Includes public presentation material on the Indico record.

  3. T. Toll, Inclusive diffraction for ep and eA at small x with Sartre, EICUG Theory Working Group seminar on diffraction, 29 April 2025.

    Discusses the inclusive-diffraction implementation, lookup tables, Pythia 8 fragmentation, and azimuthal decorrelations.

  4. J. Singh, with T. Toll, Diffractive vector meson production using Sartre with machine learning, QCD with Electron-Ion Collider II, 19 December 2022.

    Includes public presentation material on the Indico record.

  5. T. Toll, Updates on the Sartre event generator, 2019 EIC User Group Meeting, 25 July 2019.

    Covers exclusive processes, UPC extensions, the Upsilon overlap, deuterium, and early inclusive-diffraction work.

  6. T. Ullrich, Sartre: A generator for diffractive physics in ep and eA, POETIC 8, 22 March 2018.

  7. T. Toll, Sartre, a dipole model generator for the Electron Ion Collider, 9th International Workshop on Multiple Partonic Interactions at the LHC, 14 December 2017.

  8. T. Ullrich, Sartre, a generator for diffractive vector meson production in ep and eA collisions, DIS 2015, 29 April 2015.

    Includes public presentation material on the Indico record.
